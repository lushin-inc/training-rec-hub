Aaron Prickel · MasteryHow Do You Know You Have Enough
Light bulb

Reps see that "enough" isn't a gut feeling -- each step of the sales process has concrete markers (a real Time/Agenda/Outcome, the What/Why/Impact of Pain, Willing/Able on Budget, a clear Decision process) that tell you objectively whether you've earned the right to move to Fulfillment.

Who should watch

Reps present a solution and ask for the close, then get stalled, because they never actually confirmed pain, budget, or decision process along the way.
