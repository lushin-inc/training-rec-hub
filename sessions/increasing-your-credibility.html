Aaron Prickel · MasteryIncreasing Your Credibility
Light bulb

Reps realize deals are won or lost by inches — a pause after pain, a flipped question, productive pushback, clear money language — and those small choices are what make a prospect see them as credible.

Who should watch

A rep runs a solid call by the book but still loses to a competitor, without noticing the small moments that cost them credibility.
