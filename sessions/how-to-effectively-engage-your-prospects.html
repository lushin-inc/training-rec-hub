Light bulb

Reps realize the best presentation is the one the prospect never sees, because a well-built string of 'I don't know' questions does more to build their case and create urgency than any pitch could — the prospect convinces themselves.

Who should watch

A rep gives a strong, informative presentation, but the prospect stays lukewarm because they were never confronted with what they didn't know they didn't know.
