Light bulb

Reps realize genuine listening isn't passive — it requires curiosity and building the next question directly off what the prospect just said, rather than working from assumptions or a pre-planned script.

Who should watch

A rep walks away from a call able to repeat back what the prospect said, but never actually worked off any of it, because they were already thinking about their next question instead of truly listening.
