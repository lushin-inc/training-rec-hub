Aaron Prickel · MasteryMini UFCs
Light bulb

The size of the Up-Front Contract should match the size of the information or decision on the table ('lil info, lil UFC... lotsa info, lotsa UFC') — mini-UFCs prevent the trap of assuming people are 'ready' or already following along.

Who should watch

Reps set one big upfront contract at the start of a call, then barrel through everything after without checking in, and lose the prospect along the way.
