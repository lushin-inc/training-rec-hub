Light bulb

Email should only be used for quick lead response, exchanging data, and scheduling — not for selling — and even a 'ghosted' deal can be revived with a short, non-needy email that gives the prospect an easy out.

Who should watch

A prospect goes dark after a good conversation, and the rep doesn't know how to use email to get them back to the table instead of just sending another status-check message.
