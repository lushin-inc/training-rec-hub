Aaron Prickel · MasteryAwkward Sales Transitions
Light bulb

Coaches realize clunky transitions almost always trace back to weak upstream work (a shallow UFC or under-developed pain) or to not truly listening -- the fix isn't a better line, it's anchoring every transition to what the prospect just said, so the shift from step to step becomes invisible to them.

Who should watch

A rep can run every individual step of the sales process but the call still feels jerky and scripted the moment they have to move from one step to the next.
